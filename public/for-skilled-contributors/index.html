For skilled contributors

Find startup work where your skills matter

MornAI helps skilled people discover startup opportunities and connect with founders who need their expertise. Make your skills and goals clear, explore the work a startup needs, and discuss whether the collaboration is right for you.

Explore MornAI Building a startup?

Use your skills to find a suitable startup

1. Explain what you can do

Describe your real skills, experience, interests and working preferences so founders can understand how you may contribute.

2. Explore defined opportunities

Review startup context, role requirements, responsibilities and expected outcomes rather than relying on a job title alone.

3. Connect with founders

Start a conversation to ask questions about the product, team, priorities, time commitment and the work you would own.

4. Agree on terms first

Discuss scope, deadlines, payment, equity or milestone arrangements clearly before accepting work or sharing sensitive information.

Who can use MornAI?

MornAI is designed for people with useful startup skills, including software developers, designers, marketers, product specialists, writers, video editors and other contributors. You do not need to describe yourself as an expert in everything; a specific, honest profile helps a founder judge the fit.

Early-stage work can involve uncertainty and different compensation models. Read the opportunity carefully, ask questions, and make sure the expectations and terms suit you.

Keep your work connected to the startup

Beyond introductions, MornAI brings startup context, roadmaps, tasks and AI-assisted planning tools into one workspace. These tools can help people understand the company's direction and decisions, while the team remains responsible for evaluating information and doing the work.

Questions contributors ask

Does MornAI guarantee paid work?

No. The platform helps people discover opportunities and connect with founders, but it cannot guarantee that an opportunity will lead to paid work or a partnership.

Could a startup offer equity instead of immediate payment?

Some opportunities may discuss equity, milestone-based arrangements, hourly payment or other terms. Review the specific role and get a clear written agreement before starting.

What should I check before joining a startup?

Clarify the work scope, expected hours, decision-making, payment or equity terms, deadlines and how either side can end the arrangement. Do your own due diligence before committing.
